# Fiona Huang — portfolio (static site)

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder as-is.

## Editing without code (Pages CMS)
Text, links, photos, projects and the "what I'm listening to" song live in `content/*.json`, and the page fills itself from them when it loads (`assets/js/content.js`). The editor is [Pages CMS](https://app.pagescms.org): sign in with GitHub, open the `fiohuang-portfolio` repo, and pick a page (Home, About me, Experience, My works, Contact, Site settings). Saving commits to GitHub and Netlify republishes in about a minute. `.pages.yml` defines the forms; `netlify.toml` tells Netlify to publish the repo root with no build.

- The HTML in `index.html` is still the fallback: if the content files can't load, the page shows the text already written there. When you change a project/text in the editor it isn't copied back into the HTML (except the title and link-preview text, which `.github/workflows/sync-meta.yml` copies so link previews work).
- Not in the editor (change in `index.html` / CSS): the "I'm Fiona!" headline, the five vinyl tracks and their names, the ring text, the page eyebrows ("02 / WHO AM I?"), the header links, colours and decorations.
- Never put a phone number in `content/`, `assets/` or the résumé PDF.

## How it works
The site is **not one long scroll**. It's five full-screen pages, shown one at a time:

| Page | URL hash | What's on it |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `#home` | Two-line “I’m / Fiona” headline, the intro line, a rotating “I’m interested in . . .” line, and the half-visible pink vinyl (the table of contents) with a static text ring |
| about me | `#about` | Short bio, two album-style photo frames for your pictures, and a few facts |
| experience | `#experience` | A short line under the title, then ruled rows for current education and roles (University of British Columbia, Faculty of Arts (Interested in Cognitive Systems & Commerce), Vice President Graphic Design & Social Media, UBC Vietnamese Student Association, freelance photographer, private English tutor) |
| my works | `#works` (or `#works/vsa`, `#works/uv-webcam-particles`, `#works/sports-photography`) | One project at a time. The first, UBC VSA Social Media Identity, is a gallery (a sideways strip of posters + an Instagram reel link); the second, UV Webcam Particles, is a video with a downloadable TouchDesigner file. The third, Sports Photography, is a gallery of eight portrait action photos |
| contact / learn more | `#contact` | Friendly line, email, LinkedIn and résumé (PDF) links, summer 2027 internship note |

**The vinyl is the navigation.** Its five tracks (01 home, 02 about me, 03 experience, 04 my works, 05 contact / learn more) are links. The page labels ("02 / WHO AM I?" etc.) use the same numbers. Clicking one spins the disc (a full, eased turn, about 1.2s) so that track lands at the 9 o'clock position, while the old page softly blurs/fades out and the new one fades in. The disc stays on every page as a smaller half-disc so you can always spin to another page. The small home icon and the "Fiona Huang" name in the header both go home, and the browser's back/forward buttons work (back spins the disc the other way).

- **Reduced motion:** if the visitor's system has "reduce motion" on, the disc doesn't spin and pages just do a short fade.
- **Keyboard:** tracks are normal links (Tab / Enter) with a visible focus state.
- **Phones:** the half-disc peeks in from the right edge on the home screen. On the other pages it shrinks to the bottom-right corner (tap it to go back to the contents) and a small text menu appears under the name. The page scrolls inside itself only if the content is taller than the screen.

## Vinyl hover "pop"
The hover is one layered motion (real mouse/trackpad only; switched off during the intro and while a page is spinning):
- **Record pop** – hover anywhere on the record, labels included: the whole disc (with all its labels, as one piece) glides ~26px toward the page and swells ~3.5% (~.45s, smooth ease-out, no bounce). On inner pages the smaller disc glides ~14px. The record stays popped for as long as the pointer is over the disc, any label, or the small invisible halo around the disc, so it never dips back when you move from the record onto a label.
- **Word pop (additional)** – hover a track label: the record stays popped exactly as it was, and that label additionally slides ~9px further out along its own radial line, scales ~7% and becomes a soft pink pill (~.22s); the other labels dim slightly. Moving back to the surface releases only the label; leaving the disc releases both. Keyboard focus on a label gives the same layered effect.
- **Why it doesn't flicker** – hover is never read from something that moves: an invisible `.vinyl-hit` circle (never moves) plus the disc and every label keep `.vinyl-wrap:hover` true; the record pop is applied to the inner `.vinyl-pop` wrapper; each label's link (`.track a`) never moves and has an invisible extension past the text, and only its inner `.track__in` (the visible pill) does the word pop. Pure CSS, no JS.

They use the independent CSS `translate` / `scale` properties, so they layer on the intro, the click spin and the page transitions. With "reduce motion" there's no movement, only the colour highlight. Edit in the "Layered hover pop" block of `style.css`.

## Intro animation (first visit)
On the first load of the site in a browser session, the vinyl starts large and centred, then pans out to its half-visible spot at the side while spinning about half a turn and slowing as it settles. The header, headline, intro text and captions then fade up one after another (about 2.3s in total). Only `transform` and `opacity` are animated, and every animation ends on the page's normal styles, so there's no jump when it hands over to the normal spin. The tracks aren't clickable until it ends; navigating early skips straight to the end.
- It plays **once per session** (a `sessionStorage` flag called `fh-intro`) and **only when the site is opened on the home page** (no `#…` or `#home`). Opening a link like `…/#about` skips it. With "reduce motion" on, it's skipped.
- To replay while testing: clear session storage (or open a new tab/private window). To turn it off, delete the small `<script>` in the `<head>` of `index.html` that adds the `intro` class. Timings are in `playIntro()` in `assets/js/main.js` (`DUR` and the delays).

## Background decorations
A fixed layer (`<div class="bg-deco">` at the top of `<body>`) holds two very soft lavender/periwinkle blobs and nine small shapes (rings, sparkles, dots, plus signs, a squiggle) in soft periwinkle / lavender / lilac tones (plus one pink dot for warmth). They drift very slowly (18–44 s loops, CSS transform/opacity only, no JavaScript), sit behind all content, ignore the pointer, and are hidden from screen readers. They are a bit stronger on home and at about 60% strength on the other pages; on phones (<700px) some are hidden; with “reduce motion” they are still. Positions and speeds are the `.d1` … `.d9` rules (`--x`, `--y`, `--w` size, `--o` opacity, `--dur` loop length) at the bottom of `style.css`. **To remove them completely, delete the `<div class="bg-deco">` block in `index.html`** (the CSS can stay).

## Record turns on hover
While the pointer is over the record (mouse / trackpad only), the record surface itself (pink disc, grooves, sheen and centre label with its two small notches) turns slowly and continuously: about **14 s per lap**, linear, transform only (`discTurn` keyframes and the `.vinyl-disc` rule in `style.css`; change `14s` to speed it up or slow it down). The track labels and their pills, and the ring text, do **not** turn: the surface is its own layer (`<i class="vinyl-disc">` inside `.vinyl`), separate from the labels (`.tracks`). When the pointer leaves, the disc pauses at its current angle instead of snapping back, and it resumes from there on the next hover. It is off during the first-load intro and while the click spin is playing, off on touch screens, and off with “reduce motion”. The click spin (a full turn of the whole wheel, labels included) is unchanged. To remove the effect, delete the `@media (hover:hover) and (prefers-reduced-motion:no-preference)` block above `@keyframes discTurn`.
